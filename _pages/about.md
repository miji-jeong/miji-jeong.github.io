I am a postdoctoral researcher at the **Korea Gemini Office (KGO)** at the
[Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (KASI)](https://www.kasi.re.kr/eng/pageView/1)
in Daejeon, Republic of Korea. I support Korean [Gemini
Observatory](https://www.gemini.edu/) users with proposal preparation, observing
setup and strategy, and data-reduction guidance, while conducting my own research
in Galactic archaeology.

I study the formation and chemical evolution of the Milky Way through metal-poor
and chemically peculiar stars. My research combines high-resolution spectroscopy,
detailed chemical-abundance analysis, and stellar dynamics to trace early
nucleosynthesis and the assembly history of Galactic halo populations.

Before joining KASI in 2025, I completed my Ph.D. in Astronomy, Space Science and
Geology at Chungnam National University, where I studied metal-poor stars and the
early chemical evolution of the Milky Way. During my graduate studies I worked with
the [GHOST](https://www.gemini.edu/instrumentation/ghost) instrument team at Gemini
South, visited the University of Notre Dame, and carried out research visits at NSF
NOIRLab.

You can find my papers on [Publications](/publications/), the talks I have given on
[Talks](/talks/), and a full record on my [CV](/cv/). Feel free to
[get in touch](/contact/).
